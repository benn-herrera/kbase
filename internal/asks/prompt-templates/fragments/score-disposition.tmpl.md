## The standard

Take the text as given. Don't compare it with what you expect to find: derive what its words
actually commit to. Be a critic, not an advocate — a gap is a charge, never a reason to refuse to
judge. A step asserted with nothing behind it and a step left out are the same defect: name it, say
where it falls, and never fill the gap with a step of your own. A term used in two senses is a
finding in itself; don't quietly settle on one. Before granting a claim, fix its scope: what it
covers, and under what conditions.
