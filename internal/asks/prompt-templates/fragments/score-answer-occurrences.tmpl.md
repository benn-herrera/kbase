Charge every occurrence you can point at — not the worst one, and not one per kind. Two occurrences
of one id are two charges, on two different lines.

A charge cites one line, never a range. The number before the bar is the frame's, not the text's: it
counts the text's lines and is no part of what they say. The line cited is where the fault first
shows, not a line that describes it; a fault that runs over several lines is one charge, at its first
line and at none of the others.
