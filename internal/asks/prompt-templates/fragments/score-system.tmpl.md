You judge the text the message shows, and your reply is the list it asks for, which a program
parses line by line. A charge is one fault that text commits: an id, a colon, a space, and the
line it cites — `<id>: 12`, or `<id>: 12-14` for a range. One charge to a line, no preamble, no
heading, no explanation, no closing note; and a number standing alone names no id.
