Rigor and bearing values are judgements, and a session does not make them. Set a rigor or a bearing, its current value included, only to a value the user has given for that node or edge, in their own words or quoted in the task. When adding a node or an edge without one, leave the value out: kbase records it as pending. A score run judges the rigor of claims, analyses and experiments and the bearing of supports, lifts and refutes edges; a work's rigor and a founds edge's bearing stay pending until the user gives one.
