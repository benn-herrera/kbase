#### Content

The export reads every document the navigation tree names, `overview.md` included, as ordinary leaf content; `references.md` leaves are read as bibliography data, never as documents. Author labels are namespaced per volume, so a label repeated across volumes stays distinct, and LaTeX numbers what they label. A reference naming a label its volume defines resolves to it; a cleveref-family anchor (`ref+label`, `ref+Label`) renders as one `\cref`/`\Cref` over every label it names, one or several, without the anchor's own text beside it. A link to an exported KB document becomes a hyperlink to that document's heading. Prose is exported as written: a hand-maintained KB's prose mentions of equations and works (`(eq:dv)`, `(Khalil, 2002)`) and its bold-line theorem blocks stay text. The bibliography holds `references.md`'s entries or, where a volume has none, the KB's work records.

**Simplification, not refusal** (the `dot` posture defined in [§3](../../3-compatibility-contract/index.md)): a reference whose label its volume does not define, a link to a document not exported, and a citation with no bibliography entry each become the text they contain; an image whose file is not in the KB becomes its alternative text; a figure or table the export cannot rebuild, or one LaTeX cannot float, is written as far as it can be; and maths the export cannot recover from the page is left out. Each gets a report line in the export report, `gen-tex/kbase-export-report.yaml` ([§7](../../7-tool-result-contract-and-exit-codes/index.md)'s `report` key). Idempotent: a repeat over the tool's own previous export leaves the export byte-identical and reports `unchanged`. The export takes no KB write lock — nothing under `kb-root/` is written, so it reads as the queries do — and it runs no git. Result keys: [§7](../../7-tool-result-contract-and-exit-codes/index.md).
