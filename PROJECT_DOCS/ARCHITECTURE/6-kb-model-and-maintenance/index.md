## 6. KB model and maintenance

| Mechanism | Package | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Metadata layer**: kb-root model: names, exclusions, link primitives and the dead-link gate over `kb-root/`; the link grammar — an inline link with its title, a citation's id, a bare id (`LinkRE`, `Links`, `BareIDs`), defined by `format400` and re-exported here, the one pattern the dead-link gate, the tree's link readers and the converter read links through (SPEC §10); the links in a document's text (`TextLinks`: every inline link outside code, with its line and its offsets on the raw line; `TextLinkDefinitions`: every reference definition outside code, with its destination's offsets), the citations among them (`TextCitations`: each whose title is a node id) and the citing documents (`CitingDocuments`: every Markdown document but those standing at a summary page position), the one reading `citations-of`, `remove-node`'s refusal, the edits' renaming and every op's re-pointing share (SPEC §8); the uses of a citation key as a key (`CitationKeyRefs`, `bibliography.go`: a citation span's `data-cites` token and key-only rendering, a reference-list entry's id, a link's `#ref-` fragment, outside code, a blockquote's prefixes read past), which a work's label edit and a work's removal read (SPEC §8); the record loader (`LoadGraph`, the two record files decoded and encoded by `format400` and re-exported here); the statement-marker reader and the hosts it derives (`MarkerLines`, `Hosts`); a hosted node's statement as the layout around its marker gives it (`Statement`, SPEC §8 `add-citation`); the one "no node" refusal's text and the query its remedy points at (`NoNode`, `NodeLister`, beside `CheckUnknownID`); every heading's anchor in a document (`HeadingAnchors`, through `format400`'s slugger, `Slugify` and `Slugger`, re-exported here; SPEC §10); the document kind (`DocumentKind`, from the child list); the format stamp and its file; schema; the register pages' rule — their file name, title and entry headings (`RegisterFile`, `SummaryTitle`, `SummaryEntryHeadings`), the volumes among a KB's documents (`Volumes`), the page a host's entry stands on (`SummaryPage`) and every path a page can stand at (`SummaryPagePositions`: kb-root's and each volume's) — defined by `format400` and re-exported here, the one rule index renders by, the build rebases titles by and the converter predicts; the derived-line renderers the register pages are written through | `internal/kb` | Landed |
| **Write API**: render, values, store, ops — every write a batch, every op editing the records and the statement markers (`records.go`, `marks.go`), the batch's derivation computed in memory and written with them (`ops.go`) | `internal/write` | Landed. An add (`add-<kind>`, `adds.go`, SPEC §8) adopts nothing: it refuses, against the records as the batch's earlier entries left them, a title its kind holds (`titleHolder`) and a label a work or an axiom of its kind holds (`labelHolder`), then mints, for every kind, and places a hosted node's marker through `markAtLine` once `checkNamedLine` has refused a line past the end, a fence's opening or closing line and a line without the snippet. A hosted add and `add-work` run through `keepSummaryLinks`, which re-points every link into a summary entry whose repeat suffix the new entry shifts, from `anchored` before and after; `summaryMayShift` first rules out, without a derivation, an entry whose slug, its repeat suffixes stripped, no other title and no fixed rigor or strength heading shares, so a build's many adds derive nothing extra. `add-axiom` appends after every heading of `axioms.md` and shifts none; `add-axiom` marks the one unmarked heading of its text in `axioms.md` or appends its marked heading, refusing two unmarked headings of its text, `axioms.md` carried with the leaves as a `leafEdit`. An edit (`edit-<kind>`, `edits.go`, SPEC §8) replaces the fields its entry gives through `nodesOf`, each kind's records as their editable fields; an add and an edit judge a title's uniqueness within its kind by one lookup, `titleHolder`, and an edit a label's by `labelHolder`. A title or a label change (`rename`) reads each work's and axiom's link target and every node's summary entry before and after it from `index.Derive` over a source holding the batch's records and documents as its earlier entries left them (`anchored`, one derivation each side), then rewrites in every document but the summary pages, each document it changes a `leafEdit` like a leaf; `edit-axiom` rewrites its marked heading in `axioms.md`. The naming form takes the new title through `renameCitations`, which refuses at `title`, naming the citing `path:line`, a rewritten link `readsAsOneLink` does not see whole; a work's new label replaces every use of the old key as a key (`rekey`, over `kb.CitationKeyRefs`), after `refuseKeyInUse` has refused a label a span or an entry already uses. Those rewrites change lines in place, so `movedHeadings` finds each heading whose anchor they moved by comparing `kb.HeadingAnchors` line by line, before the entry and after it, and `summaries` adds each summary entry whose anchor moved; `repoint` then gives every link outside code, a citation or not, an inline link or a reference definition, one destination, computed from the one it had before the entry: the node's new target where a citation of it pointed at the old, otherwise the moved heading's or entry's new anchor, only the fragment changing (`movedAnchors`). One map, read once per link, so no link is re-pointed twice and a shifted repeat suffix (`on-x-1` → `on-x`) is never chained. The edge writes (`add-edge`, `edit-edge`, `remove-edge`, `restore-demoted`, `edges.go`, SPEC §8) look an edge's ends up in the batch's records (`edgeEnds`, refusing through `noNode`, the one "no node" refusal the edits share) and its identity (`holds`, `noEdge`); a scoring edge's cycle check is `closesCycle`, whose premise graph is built once per batch and cleared by every op that takes a scoring edge out (`dropEdges`, `demoteEdge`). An edge entry's `source`, `target` and `relation` are read from its values once it has applied; `restore-demoted`'s `restored`, known only while it applies, travels on the per-entry report `applyEntries` copies into `EntryResult`. `remove-node` and `move-node` (`nodes.go`, SPEC §8) name a node by `id`. `remove-node` reads the rows of the edges it removes from `index.Derive` over the batch's records and documents (`derived`, which `targetOf` shares), drops them through `dropEdges`, takes the record and the marker out through `dropNode` inside `keepSummaryLinks`, or an axiom's headings out of `axioms.md` through `dropAxiomHeadings`, which re-points every link into a later heading whose repeat suffix the removal lowered, and then refuses a mention that outlives the node (`refuseMentions`): a citation in any document but the summary pages, walked by `eachDocument` — `kb.CitingDocuments` and each document an earlier entry of the batch created — which `renameCitations`, `rekey` and `repoint` share, for a work a citation span carrying its label where no reference-list entry has that key (`kb.CitationKeyRefs`), or a surviving record's prose field (`graphProse`). Its `removed-edges` travels on the per-entry report as `restored` does. `move-node` checks the named line through `checkNamedLine`, places the marker with `appendMarker`, takes every other occurrence out (`unmarkEverywhere`, keeping the named line), and re-points every citation through `repoint` to `targetOf`, a hosted node's host and its marker's section anchor (`index.SectionSlug`, the derivation's own). A document the batch edits is a leaf as the derivation reads it, by its child list (`leafAt`), so a citation in a leaf leaves it a leaf. `add-citation` (`excerpt.go`, SPEC §8) opens any document of the KB as a `leafEdit` (`citingDocument`), finds the one span of the named line its snippet names outside code, links and markers (`citedSpan`; a missing one refused through `snippetAbsent`, which `checkNamedLine` shares), links it to `targetOf`, refuses a link the reader would not see whole through `readsAsOneLink`, and checks a quote against `kb.Statement`, the cited node's statement read from its host leaf by the layout around its marker, the lifts edges the batch holds deciding the split layouts. A link spliced into a heading moves its anchor, so `add-citation` runs `movedHeadings` and `repoint` after the splice, every link into a moved heading re-pointed, the new link among them; its document, line and link travel on the per-entry report as `restored` does. There is one KB write lock, `write.LockKB(kbRoot)` (`lock.go`): `internal/filelock`'s advisory lock on the directory `kb-root/` sits in. The caller takes it and holds it across the whole run (SPEC §8): `cmd` from `kbload.Open` through the batch's apply, derivation and write to the result, and around `update-format`; a scoring run takes it around its one landing, from `internal/score`, and around its start refresh, from `cmd/score.go`, never across a run ([§6](index.md)'s Scoring). The ops and refresh themselves take none, and the per-directory splice locks are gone. A writer that cannot take it within the wait ([§12](../12-constants/index.md)) gets `retry`, `check: lock`, `path` the repository root — the one item `write.HeldLockItem` gives, beside `LockKB`. `verify` and the queries take none. The build takes and releases it once to wait out a write in progress ([§8](../8-ledger-state-store-and-monitoring.md)). On Windows, where no advisory lock exists, only the read-then-compare check guards against a concurrent writer |
| **Refresh and verify; the index**: the derived rows in memory (`Derive`), the cycle load error and the cycle walk (`BreakCycles`), strength, the register pages and their freshness, `update-format`'s save (`UpdateFormat`) and the pending-conversion refusal (`PendingConversion`), the bare-id check (`BareIDFindings`, SPEC §8 `verify`) and the claim-id link check, a citation's title id among the ids it checks | `internal/index` | Landed. The build's check is `Verify`; a citation is checked as every link is. `LinkFindings` hands `kb.DeadLinksIn` the register pages' derived slugs, which turns its fragment check on: a fragment into a register page is judged against those slugs, one into any other Markdown document under `kb-root/` against its heading anchors and element ids (`documentAnchors`), read once per document; `kb.DeadLinks`, which the build's document-graph and conformance gates call, checks files alone. Each `Finding` carries its own remedy: `update-format` for `index-freshness`, the empty write batch for `register-freshness` (`EmptyBatchRemedy`), but `update-format` over a KB whose conversion is pending, where the batch is refused, and a write batch of the `add-citation` entry for `bare-id`. A register page's entry anchors are computed at derive (`pageAnchors`): the page's title and every entry's headings (`kb.SummaryEntryHeadings`, defined by `format400`) through one `kb.Slugger` in page order, so a claim's and a work's canonical anchor, a page's links and the dead-link gate's page slugs read one map; a test holds the rendered page's headings to it |
| **Queries** | `internal/query` | Landed |
| **Sheet**: DOT emitter and `dot` invocation; `Derive`'s rows, the leaves and the entry point's and volumes' headings in, SVGs out | `internal/sheet` | Landed as the drawing of SPEC §3; the placeholder only where `dot` is absent |
| **Readiness stamping and README assembly** | `internal/kbdocs` | Landed: the stamp (only where absent), the maintained blocks (`Maintain`: each document's one ordered pair of fence lines, recognised by their ends, its span rewritten to the template's block; an absent `AGENTS.md` or `CONVENTIONS.md` written whole, `README.md` only where it stands, a document without the pair listed and untouched; `AGENTS.md`'s scope pin `AuthoredKBPin` where no build record stands, `init` writing none), the overview excerpts, the passage structure check, the README assembly; the templates under `internal/kbdocs/templates/` |
| **Scratch creation (`init`)**: the zero-case KB — entry point, the format stamp file, the two empty record files, readiness stamps, refresh over the skeleton, the plain-subject birth commit | `cmd` (the verb), reusing `kb`'s stamp codec and record encoder, `atomicfile`, `kbdocs.Stamp`, `index.RefreshReporting`, `write.LockKB` and `gitledger` | Landed: refuses a multi-line `--title`, outside a worktree and at any existing `kb-root/` before any write; the spine bytes come from the canonical stamp and record encoders; the stamp file stands before the loader opens the KB ([§10](../10-compatibility-harness.md)); `verify` over the born KB is `test-integration-init`'s, not the verb's; the birth commit's subject is outside `ledger.Prefix`, so [§5](../5-reader-and-transforms/index.md)'s fresh-vs-resume reads the trail as empty |
| **Scoring (`kbase score`)**: the closed text one call carries and its guard, the runner, the run's ledger, the run's entry and its landing | `internal/score` | Landed: the set is `query.ScoringSet`'s, the ledger stands under the build's state directory (`internal/statedir`, extracted from `internal/build`), and the verb is `cmd/score.go`, bound as a detached tool in MCP |
