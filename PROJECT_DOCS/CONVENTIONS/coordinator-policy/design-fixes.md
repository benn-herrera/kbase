### Design fixes

Where a line of an active plan's normative design page says less than the established answer of
databases, graphs or command-line tools, for behaviour the page already has, the coordinator
rewords that line without asking the page's owner:
- one line on one page per commit, its subject prefixed `design-fix:`;
- each listed, one line with its commit, in the next status message to the owner.

Anything else on the page waits for the owner's go: adding, removing or renaming a command, an
argument, a result key, a refusal or a KB format field; anything touching scoring; a change
spanning more than one page.
